Non-communicative
The people are not using eye gaze to interact. Their attention is independent,
even if they are physically close or speaking.

Mutual Gaze
The people make and maintain direct eye contact. Their gaze establishes an
active communication channel, such as acknowledging one another, initiating an
interaction, or remaining engaged in conversation.

Gaze Aversion
The interaction begins with eye contact, but one person deliberately looks
away. This breaks or avoids continued eye contact. The gaze shift may express
disengagement, discomfort, thoughtfulness, or an attempt to reduce intimacy.

Gaze Following
One person notices the direction of another person's gaze and looks toward the
same target. They attend to the same object or location, but there is no initial
eye contact confirming a shared intention. This is one-way observation rather
than fully established joint attention.

Joint Attention
Two people intentionally establish shared attention toward the same target.
One person directs the other person's attention, the second person follows,
and both recognize that they are sharing the experience.
